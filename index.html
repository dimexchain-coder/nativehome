DimeX Chain — Whitepaper

1. Executive Summary

DimeX Chain is an EVM-compatible blockchain network intended to provide public blockchain infrastructure for digital asset activity, wallet connectivity and ecosystem applications. The network is designed around transparent on-chain verification and familiar EVM development tools.

2. Vision and Mission

Vision: Build an accessible and dependable blockchain ecosystem that can support developers, users and digital services over time.

Mission: Maintain usable network infrastructure, improve developer access, encourage responsible ecosystem integrations and publish clear technical information.

3. Network Overview

  • Network name: DimeX Chain
  • Chain ID: 1723
  • Virtual machine compatibility: EVM
  • Consensus: QBFT
  • Native coin symbol: DMXC
  • Native coin decimals: 18
  • Public RPC: https://rpc.dimexchain.tech/
  • Block explorer: https://www.dimexscan.tech

4. Coin Supply and Genesis Allocation

The stated total coin supply is 10 billion (10,000,000,000) coins. The project's initial allocation is defined in the network genesis configuration. This website does not publish a new allocation schedule or imply that genesis allocations have been changed. The native network coin is identified by the DMXC ticker in the chain configuration.

5. Ecosystem and Utility Direction

The DimeX ecosystem includes the DimeX Chain network, DimeXScan explorer and DimeX exchange platform. Development priorities may include wallet compatibility, developer resources, application integrations and additional ecosystem services. Any future utility or integration will be described as available only after it is implemented and verified.

6. Technology and Network Operations

DimeX Chain uses an EVM-compatible execution environment and QBFT consensus. Network availability, validator operation, software maintenance and protocol upgrades are technical responsibilities that require ongoing monitoring, testing and operational controls. Users should verify transactions and network details through the official explorer.

7. Development Roadmap

  • Infrastructure: maintain the chain, RPC access and public explorer.
  • Documentation: publish network, wallet and developer guidance.
  • Ecosystem: improve exchange, wallet and application integrations where technically and legally appropriate.
  • Expansion: support developer participation, partnerships and community growth.

These are development objectives, not guarantees of delivery or fixed launch dates. Priorities and timing may change.

8. Governance, Compliance and Risk

Digital asset networks involve technical, operational, market and regulatory risks. The project will need to assess applicable legal requirements in each jurisdiction before offering regulated services or expanding exchange-related activities. No statement in this paper is legal, financial or investment advice.

9. Disclaimer

This whitepaper is informational and describes the project at a high level. It is not an offer, solicitation, promise of returns or guarantee of future performance. Digital assets can lose value, transactions may be irreversible, and users are responsible for their own wallet security and independent evaluation.
