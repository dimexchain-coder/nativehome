Compliance & Security

Privacy Policy

At DimeX Chain (dimexchain.tech), we prioritize the privacy and security of our users and network participants. As a decentralized Layer-1 blockchain infrastructure provider, we adhere to strict data protection standards.

1. RPC Endpoint Data Collection

When connecting to our public RPC endpoint (https://rpc.dimexchain.tech), standard node request parameters (such as IP addresses and transaction request headers) are temporarily processed solely to facilitate blockchain data queries, smart contract calls, and transaction broadcasting. No personal user data is logged or shared.

2. Wallet Security & Decentralization

DimeX Chain is a non-custodial blockchain network. We do not store, request, or have access to your private keys, secret recovery phrases, or personal credentials. Users retain full control over their funds at all times.
